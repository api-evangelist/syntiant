# Syntiant

> Syntiant Corp is an Irvine, California edge-AI semiconductor company founded in 2017. It designs ultra-low-power Neural Decision Processors (NDPs) and the deep-learning models and tooling that run on them, putting always-on speech, audio, sensor and vision inference into battery-powered devices without a cloud connection. In December 2024 Syntiant completed the USD 150 million acquisition of Knowles Corporation's Consumer MEMS Microphones business.

Syntiant is a silicon, module and embedded-software supplier, not an API provider. As of 2026-08-02 there is no public Syntiant web API, no OpenAPI or other machine-readable contract, no developer portal, no MCP server, no A2A agent card and no event/webhook surface. The Syntiant SDK and Training Development Kit (TDK) are embedded C/Python toolchains delivered to customers under agreement through a private GitLab, not a public developer platform.

## Products

- [Chips and Hardware](https://www.syntiant.com/hardware): The Neural Decision Processor family overview.
- [NDP100 / NDP101](https://www.syntiant.com/ndp100): Microwatt-power NDP for always-on speech interfaces in wearables, phones, speakers and laptops.
- [NDP102](https://www.syntiant.com/ndp102): NDP for edge AI sensor applications.
- [NDP120](https://www.syntiant.com/products/chips-and-hardware/ndp120/): Second-generation Syntiant Core 2 device for always-on audio and sensor fusion.
- [NDP200](https://www.syntiant.com/ndp200/): Syntiant Core 2 device targeting vision and multimodal edge workloads.
- [NDP250](https://www.syntiant.com/products/chips-and-hardware/ndp250/): Highest-throughput Syntiant NDP, ~5x the tensor throughput of the NDP120/NDP200 generation.

## Developer

- [GitHub organization](https://github.com/syntiant): Official but nearly empty - one public repository, a fork of TizenRT. No first-party client libraries are published to npm, PyPI, crates.io or any other public registry.
- [Edge Impulse: Syntiant NDP tutorials](https://docs.edgeimpulse.com/tutorials/hardware/syntiant-ndp-motion-recognition): Third-party tooling that targets Syntiant NDP hardware; the practical public on-ramp for developers.

## Notes for agents

- Do not attempt programmatic access to Syntiant. There is no published API.
- www.syntiant.com is behind a SiteGround bot wall that answers automated requests with HTTP 202 and a captcha meta-refresh shell, so crawls of the site will not return content.
- Contact: info@syntiant.com
